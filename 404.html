A Few Things to Know

Property Policy

Property Policy: Hotel guidelines strictly restrict pets on the premises. To maintain our core environment, accommodation is exclusively reserved for families and corporate executives. The management reserves the absolute right of admission, and valid verification matching our booking guidelines is mandatory for all registering guests at check-in.

Local identification

Local IDs are not accepted for check-in.

Primary guest

The primary guest must be at least 18 years old.

Identification

Accepted ID types: Passport, Aadhaar, Driving License or government ID. Please bring valid identification for check-in.

Booking through a partner? Check the check-in, checkout, payment and cancellation terms shown on your booking confirmation. Contact the front desk before booking if you need clarification.
